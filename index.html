Butter Cake

Ingredients

Salted butter (MILK), caster sugar, EGGS, fortified WHEAT flour [WHEAT flour, calcium carbonate, iron, niacin, thiamin], whole MILK, baking powder [raising agents: diphosphates, sodium carbonates; rice flour], Madagascan vanilla extract.

Allergen advice: Contains WHEAT, MILK and EGGS.

Allergen advice: Contains WHEAT, MILK, CELERY, MUSTARD and SESAME.

Herbal Coffee

Water, sugar, coffee beans, coriander, cumin, dried ginger, cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, black pepper.

Allergen advice: No regulated allergens identified from the recipe supplied. Contains caffeine.

Masala Tea

Water, whole MILK, sugar, black tea, cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, ginger.
